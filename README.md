# 🍽️ Zomato Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Zomato is a leading food delivery and restaurant discovery platform with a large network of restaurants and customers. Understanding customer preferences, ordering behavior, restaurant ratings, and spending patterns can help improve customer engagement and promotional strategies.

In this project, **Zomato restaurant and customer-ordering data is analyzed using Python** to identify meaningful patterns and trends related to restaurant preferences, customer engagement, ratings, spending behavior, and online/offline ordering patterns.

The project uses **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and data visualization to answer key business questions and generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 💼 Business Problem

Zomato wants to better understand how customers interact with different types of restaurants and ordering modes.

The analysis aims to identify:

* Which restaurant types are most preferred by customers
* How customer engagement varies across restaurant types
* What ratings restaurants receive most frequently
* How much customers spend on online orders
* Whether online or offline orders receive better ratings
* Which restaurant types receive more offline orders

These insights can help Zomato make **data-driven decisions related to customer offers, restaurant promotions, and customer engagement strategies**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Zomato data and understand customer and restaurant behavior.

The analysis focuses on:

* Restaurant types and customer preferences
* Customer votes and engagement
* Restaurant rating distribution
* Average customer spending
* Online vs. offline ordering behavior
* Offline orders across different restaurant types

---

## ❓ Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following business questions:

### 1. 🍴 Restaurant Preference

**What type of restaurant do the majority of customers order from?**

This helps identify the most popular restaurant type among customers.

### 2. 👍 Customer Engagement

**How many votes has each type of restaurant received from customers?**

This helps compare customer engagement and interaction across different restaurant types.

### 3. ⭐ Restaurant Ratings

**What ratings have the majority of restaurants received?**

This helps understand the distribution of restaurant ratings and identify the most frequently received rating.

### 4. 💰 Customer Spending

**Zomato has observed that most couples order their food online. What is their average spending on each order?**

This helps understand the average spending behavior of couples who order food online.

### 5. 📱 Online vs. Offline Ratings

**Which mode (online or offline) has received the maximum rating?**

This helps compare customer satisfaction between online and offline ordering modes.

### 6. 🏪 Offline Ordering by Restaurant Type

**Which type of restaurant receives more offline orders, so that Zomato can target customers with suitable offers?**

This helps identify restaurant types with higher offline ordering activity and can support targeted promotional strategies.

## 🔍 Analysis Approach

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

### 1. Data Loading

* Import the Zomato dataset using Pandas.
* Load the dataset into a DataFrame for analysis.

### 2. Data Understanding

* Examine the dataset structure.
* Understand columns and data types.
* Review basic statistical information.
* Identify important variables for the analysis.

### 3. Data Cleaning

* Handle missing or inconsistent data where required.
* Convert columns into appropriate data types.
* Prepare the dataset for further analysis.

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis explores:

* Restaurant type preferences
* Customer votes
* Restaurant ratings
* Customer spending patterns
* Online and offline ordering behavior
* Offline orders across restaurant types

### 5. Data Visualization

Charts and graphs are used to identify patterns and communicate the results clearly.

**Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** are used to create visualizations for different business questions.

### 6. Business Insights

The results of the analysis are interpreted from a business perspective to understand customer behavior and restaurant performance.

### 7. Conclusion

The major findings are summarized along with their potential implications for Zomato's customer engagement and promotional strategies.
